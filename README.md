# Mikołaj Leszczuk

Associate Professor at AGH University of Krakow, Institute of Telecommunications and Cybersecurity, Faculty of Computer Science, Electronics and Telecommunications.

I study how image and video impairments affect perceived quality and the usefulness of visual information. My research combines subjective experiments, no-reference quality indicators and task-based evaluation. Recent work includes underwater image enhancement, crowdsourced video experiments and audiovisual quality prediction.

Since June 2026, I have coordinated the international MUTASK consortium and led the AGH research team, working on multimodal translation and adaptation of scientific knowledge for global accessibility.

I supervise doctoral research and teach multimedia processing, telecommunications and Python in Polish and English. I am a VQEG board member and chair its QACoViA and MEHF groups.
